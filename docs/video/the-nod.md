# THE NOD — Energym promo concept

**Format:** 16:9 · 4K master · 24/30 fps · 20–30 seconds · seamless loop · muted-safe
**Style:** 2D comic / cel-shaded. Thick ink outlines, flat colour, halftone dots,
speed lines, hard panel cuts. Energym palette: ink black, newsprint cream, acid
green `#54FF2F`, acid yellow `#E4FF1A`, hot pink `#FF2D6F`.
**Not:** photoreal, 3D render, anime, GTA/Rockstar, any existing IP.

## The idea

You don't join a gym. You get **nodded in**.

A nervous first-timer pushes through the door. The crew doesn't pitch. They
don't do a fake smile. One trainer looks up and gives a single nod. Then
another. Then the whole room. The rookie nods back. That's it. They're in.

The nod is the membership card. It's the meme. It's the thing people copy at
the door, in the car, in the group chat.

**Tagline:** *You don't join a gym. You get nodded in.*
**End card:** *ENERGYM. JOIN THE CREW.*

## Text overlays

Keep it to four hits, all huge, all readable from across a gym floor:

1. **FIRST DAY.**
2. **NO INDUCTIONS. JUST NODS.**
3. **YOU'RE IN.**
4. **ENERGYM. JOIN THE CREW.**

No small print. No phone number. No QR code on the big screen.

## Master style prompt

> 2D comic-book animation, cel-shaded, thick black ink outlines, flat colour,
> halftone dot shading, high contrast, dramatic low-angle composition, speed
> lines, subtle paper grain. Palette: ink black, newsprint cream, acid green
> #54FF2F, acid yellow #E4FF1A, hot pink #FF2D6F. Cinematic comic panel framing,
> bold shadows, graphic lighting. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not anime, no
> existing game or film IP.

## Character sheet

**The Rookie** — fictional, not a real staff member. Mid-20s, mixed heritage,
short dark curly hair, black hoodie, gym bag, nervous but determined. Arc:
small at the door → nods back → belongs in the group shot. Keep the same face,
hoodie and bag in every shot.

**The Crew** — use the real Energym trainer portraits as reference, illustrated
in the same comic treatment. Keep likenesses recognisable and consistent. The
nod wave should include Callum, Carla, Martyna, Shella, and a few others from
the real roster.

## Shot prompts (image-to-video)

Use one keyframe per shot, then animate. Keep the same style prompt at the top
of every generation.

**Shot 1 — The door**
> Start on a black frame with a thin blade of acid-green light. A heavy gym door
> pushes open from the inside; green light spills across a dark floor. Low
> camera. Comic-book style, thick ink outlines, halftone shadows. Slow push-in.
> No people yet.

**Shot 2 — The room**
> A nervous rookie in a black hoodie and gym bag steps through the door into a
> huge dark gym. The crew trains in silhouette against green and pink neon.
> Nobody stops. Wide shot, slow push-in, comic-book ink and halftone. The rookie
> is small in the frame.

**Shot 3 — The details**
> A rapid comic montage of details: chalked hands clapping in a cloud of chalk, a
> weight plate sliding onto a bar, a battle rope rippling, narrowed eyes, a
> chalked hand gripping a bar. Macro shots, hard cuts, high contrast, ink lines,
> halftone, green and pink rim light.

**Shot 4 — The nod**
> A muscular trainer looks up from a squat rack and gives one slow, deliberate
> nod. Cut to a woman with glasses giving the same nod. Cut to another trainer,
> then a wide shot of the whole crew nodding in sequence like a wave. Comic-book
> close-ups, green rim light, halftone shading, serious expressions, no smiling
> yet.

**Shot 5 — The nod back**
> The rookie, still holding the gym bag, exhales and nods back once. A giant
> comic stamp slams over the frame: YOU'RE IN. Medium close-up, ink outlines,
> halftone, green light on one side of the face, pink on the other.

**Shot 6 — The montage**
> Quick comic panels of the rookie training with the crew: spotting a bench
> press, a high-five, riding a spin bike under green lights, laughing in a
> huddle, walking through the gym with the crew. Match cuts, speed lines,
> halftone, bold ink, energetic camera.

**Shot 7 — The crew**
> The full crew stands shoulder to shoulder in a comic-book group shot, arms
> crossed, the rookie in the middle. Green and pink lighting, halftone, thick
> ink, confident expressions. Slow push-in, then freeze frame. Energym logo end
> card.

## Negative prompt

> photorealistic, 3D render, CGI, anime, manga screentones, GTA, Rockstar,
> Grand Theft Auto, existing game characters, existing film characters, logos
> other than Energym, distorted faces, extra fingers, warped equipment, floating
> limbs, text artifacts, watermark, low contrast, muddy shadows.

## Sound design

- Door thud, low sub-bass rumble.
- Chalk clap, plate clank, rope whip, distant gym murmur.
- A single 808 hit when the stamp lands on **YOU'RE IN.**
- Music: dark, cool, restrained. Builds through the nod wave, drops on the
  stamp, rides the montage, cuts to the group shot.
- The film must work muted. The text and the nod carry the story.

## Production notes

- Draft at 720p on Wan 3.0 or Kling 4.0 Flash. Final at 1080p/4K on Gemini Omni
  Flash 1.2, Kling 4.0 or Seedance 2.5.
- Use reference-to-video for the crew. Gemini Omni Flash 1.2 supports up to six
  reference images; Kling O3 and Seedance 2.5 also have reference modes.
- Generate the seven keyframes first, lock the look, then animate. Don't burn
  credits on text-to-video and hope.
- Keep every shot 3–5 seconds. Short cuts hide AI inconsistencies and make the
  loop feel faster.
- End frame must match the opening black door frame for a seamless loop.
- Get consent from any real trainer whose likeness is used. For the pitch, keep
  the crew stylised and clearly illustrative.
- No fake staff, no fake testimonials, no fake claims. The cool-crowd feeling
  comes from the nod, not from inventing people.

## Alternate hooks (if you want a different flavour)

**THE CREW CARD** — each trainer gets a comic trading card with a class and a
speciality. The rookie's card prints at the end: *YOU. ROOKIE. DAY ONE.* Very
shareable, great for social cutdowns, slightly more graphic and less emotional.

**THE DOOR** — a surreal loop where every time the door opens, a different
version of the same person walks in and is greeted by the crew. Ends on *YOUR
SEAT'S SAVED.* More mysterious, less literal, harder to land in 20 seconds.

## Why "The Nod" fits Energym

- It makes membership about belonging, not facilities.
- It gives the crew a personality without dialogue.
- It's a repeatable meme: the nod can become the gym's real-world greeting.
- It works on a muted screen, on a phone, and as a 6-second social cut.
- It doesn't need a celebrity, a voiceover or a fake transformation.
