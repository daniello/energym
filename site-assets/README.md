## Style notes for generating matching images

Use these when creating new team portraits / campaign imagery:

- **Setting:** inside the gym, shot on a fast lens with shallow depth of field.
  Background is racks, rigs and equipment falling into dark bokeh.
- **Lighting:** strong green/teal LED tubes and neon accents behind the subject;
  occasional magenta/blue rim light on shoulders and hair. A cool key light on
  the face with a warm skin tone.
- **Grade:** dark, high contrast, saturated. Crushed blacks, boosted greens,
  slightly cyan shadows. Almost a nightclub look applied to a gym.
- **Composition:** half-body / three-quarter portrait, centred, subject fills
  most of the frame, head near the top edge, cropped around waist to mid-thigh.
- **Pose:** arms crossed, squared shoulders, direct eye contact, relaxed smile
  or confident neutral expression.
- **Wardrobe:** black Energym polo with vertical white `ENERGYM` lettering and a
  green logo, or plain black sportswear. Minimal accessories (smart watch,
  small earrings, cap).
- **Aspect ratio:** portrait 2:3, e.g. 1226×1718 or 2806×3937.
- **Consistency:** all portraits share the same backdrop family and colour
  grade, so a new image should reuse the green-lit gym background and black kit
  rather than invent a new look.
