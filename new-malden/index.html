The floor

Six zones. One floor.

Every zone is named, stocked and ready. This is the full kit list — no stock photos, no maybes.

Gold Zone 01

Annihilate your PB with:

  • Dumbbells
  • Multi-adjustable benches
  • Incline / decline flat bench
  • T-bar row
  • Preacher curl
  • Smith machines
  • Multi-adjustable bench press

Emerald Zone 02

Push your endurance levels to their very limits using:

  • 8-station cables
  • Chest press
  • Shoulder press
  • Tricep dip
  • Lat pulldown
  • Hamstring curl
  • Calf raise
  • Leg extension
  • Abductor / adductor
  • Low row
  • Rear kick
  • Leg curl

Opal Zone 03

Supercharge your cardio stamina with:

  • Treadmills
  • Cross trainers
  • Rowers
  • Ski erg
  • Stretching area
  • Upright bikes

Onyx Zone 04

Train like a gladiator using:

  • 20 squat rack stations
  • Hip thrust
  • Abductor / adductor
  • Pendulum squat
  • 45-degree leg press
  • Power squat
  • V squat
  • Hack squat
  • Calf raise
  • Sleds
  • TRX stations
  • Pull-up / dip station

Diamond Zone 05

Jumpstart your heart-rate with extreme HIIT on:

  • Steppers
  • Rowing machines

  • 12 × 55kg punch bags

  • Medicine balls
  • Kettlebells
  • Barbell sets
  • Battle rope
  • Running track
  • Sleds
